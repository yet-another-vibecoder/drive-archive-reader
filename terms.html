Purpose

TypingMind Drive Read Only is an unofficial personal connector for user-directed, read-only access to Google Drive and Gmail. This website describes a locally operated installation; it does not supply a public hosted service.

Your controls

Use only accounts and material you are authorized to access. You choose the connected Google account, the requests sent through TypingMind and the AI provider receiving tool results. Review the privacy policy and the applicable Google, TypingMind and AI-provider terms before connecting or sending content.

Limitations

Search can be incomplete. Text extraction, OCR and AI-generated summaries can contain errors or omit information; check important results against the source. Service availability, model behavior and authorization duration depend partly on third-party services. The connector is provided as available, without a promise of uninterrupted operation or error-free results. Nothing here limits rights or responsibilities that applicable law does not permit to be limited.

Ending use

You can stop using the connector at any time, disable its tools in TypingMind and revoke its Google access. Previously retained chats and provider-held copies require separate management as described in the privacy policy.

The connector and these pages may be updated when functionality or data flows change. The effective date identifies this version.
