Privacy policy

Application: TypingMind Drive Read Only, a personal connector that also supports Gmail. Effective date: 24 September 2026.

Access and purpose

The account owner grants read-only access to Google Drive and, separately, Gmail to search, retrieve and summarize information they request in TypingMind. These permissions cover the accessible contents of the authorized account; they are not restricted to one folder or label. The connector does not request cloud write permissions.

Data processed

Depending on the requested tool, processed data can include file names and metadata, document text, message headers and bodies, thread information, labels, existing drafts, and attachment metadata. Drive PDF and EML readers temporarily download a local copy to extract text; normal completion and handled failures remove the temporary copy. A process crash or forced shutdown can leave temporary files that require local cleanup.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens and the OAuth client configuration are stored on the owner's computer in files restricted to that operating-system user. Drive and Gmail tokens are stored separately. Reconnection can create a local backup of prior tokens. These files are not uploaded to this website.

Tool results are supplied to TypingMind and may be included in requests to the AI provider chosen by the user. Chat content may be retained by TypingMind in local storage, cloud sync, backups or exports according to the user's settings. The selected provider's retention and training policies apply to data the user sends to that provider. The connector cannot promise zero retention or disable a provider's practices on the user's behalf.

Use limitations

The connector itself does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or run an independent analytics or model-training service. Google user data is used to provide the user-requested reading and analysis features, consistent with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Controls and deletion

The owner can disable a connector in TypingMind and revoke the application's access in their Google Account permissions. Removing local token files and their backups removes stored connector credentials. Deleting a connector does not delete existing chats or copies held by an AI provider; those must be managed through the relevant service. Drive and Gmail share an OAuth client in this personal setup, so revoking the application's Google grant can affect both connections.

This website

These static pages contain no sign-in form, analytics scripts or tracking pixels. The hosting provider may process ordinary web-request information under its own privacy policy. The website has no access to the local connector or the owner's Google data.

Contact

This is a private personal installation. Its operator can be reached through the support email shown on its Google authorization screen. Do not post credentials, mail or private documents in public support discussions.
